## 👋 About me

I'm a geoinformatics engineer who moved into data and AI through **BeCode Brussels**, then spent three months as a **Data Science Intern at Sirris – Innovation Forward**, benchmarking time series foundation models (Chronos-2, TimesFM, TiRex and others) against classical baselines on real industrial data.

When I started at Sirris I knew nothing about time series foundation models. Three months later I had built the experiment pipeline in Docker, stress-tested the models on messy data, and written the technical report. Learning fast on unfamiliar ground is what I'm best at.

Most of my projects go end to end: pulling messy data from websites, public APIs or satellite images, checking it before anyone relies on it, and ending in a dashboard, an API or a forecast.
